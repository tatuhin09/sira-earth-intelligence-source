How SIRA separates autonomy from authority.

SIRA is a persistent Self-Improving Research Agent. Its purpose is to ask questions, gather evidence, retain verified knowledge and test possible improvements without letting a candidate certify itself.

01

Problem and solution

An autonomous assistant can mistake a discovered source for established knowledge or a test pass for general ability. SIRA uses distinct states for source evidence, verified claims, practice results and promoted improvements. Its unified Core records why each bounded task went to a subsystem.

02

Architecture

One SIRA identity connects a Core and shared state to research, memory, learning, engineering and workers. Direct owner requests take priority over background work. The public site receives only a static, allowlisted snapshot; it cannot dispatch tasks back into SIRA.

03

Research → verification → memory

Source discovery begins a question. Readable evidence is extracted into narrow candidate claims, checked for relevance and independent support, and assessed for contradictions and freshness. Only claims passing the applicable gate reach verified memory. Memory-first answering reuses only sufficiently relevant, current, provenance-bound knowledge; otherwise SIRA says more research is needed.

04

Evaluation and improvement

Practice execution and independent practice verification are separate. Repeated distinct successes are required before a skill can become active. Engineering candidates are isolated and evaluated against deterministic, hidden and fresh cases. An independent evaluator, a protected final gate, authorization and rollback remain distinct steps. Passing a benchmark is evidence, not permission.

05

Provenance

Knowledge records retain source and evidence references, verification decisions, freshness classes and relevant lifecycle history. This public overview reveals counts and capability states without publishing the private evidence, claim text, task identifiers or implementation.

06

Safety and authority

Owner Start/Stop, promotion authorization, protected evaluation, secret handling and no-paid-spending default stay in the private system. The public competition artifact is static HTML, CSS and a restricted JSON snapshot. It has no chat, mutation API, owner controls, repository browser or network path into a private runtime.

07

Current limitations and roadmap

Current evidence demonstrates bounded verified claims, local retrieval and release acceptance. It does not justify universal knowledge, general research mastery, or unrestricted autonomous deployment. Future work may expand independent evaluation, coverage and long-duration reliability only after measured checks. The snapshot may become outdated; its capture time is shown on the overview.
